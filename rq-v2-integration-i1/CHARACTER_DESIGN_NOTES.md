# RQV2 — Character and Power Design Notes

## Future three-star character concepts

### Controlled movement without match resolution
**Status:** Design backlog only; not implemented.

A future three-star character may have a movement power that offers a deliberate choice between:
- **Resolve:** reposition tiles, then trigger matches and cascades normally.
- **Reposition only:** reposition tiles without resolving matches or cascades, allowing strategic setup for a later move.

This is intentionally **not** a Pip (one-star) ability. Pip's **Scurry Up** and **Sideways Swarm** use two-tap origin/destination targeting, with selectable distances from 1 through the level's maximum (currently capped at 3). Both always resolve matches and cascades after a successful move, including level 4.

Consider the future three-star character's energy cost, timing and enemy-turn interaction before implementing the optional no-resolution mode.

### Precision trap placement
**Status:** Design backlog only; not implemented.

A future three-star trap specialist could choose trap locations, unlike Iris's two-star random trap placement.
