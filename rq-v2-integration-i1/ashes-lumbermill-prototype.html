🔥 The Burning Lumbermill

Embers from the Thornclaw raid have ignited the village lumbermill. Collect water and coordinated-work resources before the timber is consumed.

Mill integrity100
Water / Work0 / 0
Turn1

Swap adjacent tiles to match at least three of a color. Blue matches supply water; green matches organize firefighting crews. Reach 12 blue and 8 green collected tiles to save the mill. Fire spreads every 3 turns, damages integrity each turn, and consumes its tile until extinguished. Matching blue adjacent to fire extinguishes it. Orange sawdust-marked tiles ignite more readily. This prototype deliberately isolates the hazard mechanics from character powers and cloud rewards.
