# RQV2 — Three-star character design ideas

## Player-selected trap placement (future design; not implemented)

**Concept:** A three-star trap specialist can select the exact board tiles where traps are placed, rather than relying on random placement.

**Rarity distinction:** Iris is a two-star character and her **Delayed Danger** power must continue to place traps randomly among eligible tiles. Targeted placement is reserved as a potential three-star character mechanic, providing strategic control without changing Iris's existing role.

**Design considerations for future implementation:**
- Show eligible trap destinations with the standard tile-targeting overlay and let the player select the required number of tiles.
- Preserve ordinary tile-match colors underneath traps and avoid illegal targets or occupied special tiles.
- Consider the tradeoff between targeted placement and trap damage, duration, number of traps, and energy cost.
- Evaluate synergy with enemy match prediction, forced tile swaps, and trap-trigger multipliers.
- Exact character identity, balance, and power levels remain undecided.

**Status:** Design backlog only. Do not implement until explicitly approved.
