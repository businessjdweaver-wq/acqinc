Who attacks when two heroes share a color?

The available hero with the highest damage strength in that color gets the tile icon and moves to the front when that color attacks. Character level and the rank of an equipped power in that color can affect damage strength.

On an exact tie, the hero in the earlier roster slot wins. Current battlefield position doesn’t break the tie.

Example. Iris and Corvin both use blue and purple. If their damage strengths tie in both colors, the hero earlier in your roster appears on both sets of tiles.

Does the tile icon limit who can use the energy?

Energy goes into a shared team pool. Either hero can spend it on an equipped power of that color when the team has enough energy.
